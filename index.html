1. Introduction

FlexiClick ("we", "our", or "the application") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user data, permissions, and the use of the Android AccessibilityService API.

Summary: FlexiClick does NOT collect, transmit, share, sell, or store any personal information, keystrokes, messages, passwords, or device analytics. All configuration profiles and click coordinates remain strictly on your local device.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or user accounts.
  • No Keystroke Logging: We do not record keyboard input, passwords, credit card numbers, or typed text.
  • No Screen Scraping: We do not capture, record, or read text, images, or content from other apps.
  • No Tracking or Analytics: We do not track your location, browsing history, or app usage telemetry.

3. Permissions Requested & Purpose

A. Accessibility Service (android.permission.BIND_ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE)

  • Why Required: The Android AccessibilityService API is the official platform mechanism that allows FlexiClick to simulate physical screen touch taps (dispatchGesture) on your device.
  • Scope of Use: FlexiClick uses this permission only to dispatch touch click gestures at the specific screen coordinates you configure.
  • Data Guarantee: We do not read screen content, inspect window hierarchies for data extraction, or access private application states.

B. Display Over Other Apps (android.permission.SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW)

  • Why Required: Allows FlexiClick to show the floating target crosshair picker and the compact runtime status badge on top of other running applications.

C. Foreground Service (android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE)

  • Why Required: Ensures Android does not kill the automation timer loop when you minimize FlexiClick to run another application. A persistent notification is displayed whenever the foreground service is active.

D. Query All Packages (android.permission.QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES)

  • Why Required: Allows the in-app "Target Application" dialog to list installed applications so you can conveniently pick which application you wish to link to your automation profile.

4. Local Data Storage

FlexiClick stores your automation settings (tap coordinates, delay seconds, and theme preferences) locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences. This data never leaves your device and is wiped automatically if you uninstall the application or clear app data in Android settings.

5. Third-Party Services

FlexiClick does not integrate with third-party tracking, advertising SDKs, or cloud analytics services in this release.

6. Children's Privacy

FlexiClick does not address anyone under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
